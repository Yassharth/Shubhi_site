How to use this

Each subject opens into its chapters. A chapter gives you the idea in a few paragraphs, something to draw, the points worth memorising, and one worked example done step by step.

Under the chapters there is usually something to play with: a ray of light you can bend, a box of particles you can heat, a timeline you can drag. Those are there because some things are much easier to see than to read.

Then five questions, with the reasoning explained whichever way you answer.
